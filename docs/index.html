Transparency

From data to judgement

Every day the analytics engine takes in every portfolio's data, computes every measure and checks each against its limit; the risk manager sees only what it flags. The AI follows up what the engine found and drafts from it. The risk manager then works the flags through with the AI: a breakdown that explains VaR near its limit, and a pushed-back draft that turns stock selection into unintended value beta. The result is transparency: every flag traced to its numbers, explained, and decided on the record.

Drawn from the investment risk framework: the philosophy, how return is earned, what investment risk means, risk data, working with AI and the alpha and beta method, the three pillars and the integrated view, limits and escalation, controls and the redesign method.
